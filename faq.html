Frequently asked questions

Will you be our DJ, or someone from your company?

Me. When you book DJ Michael, I'm the one who shows up. I keep a network of DJs I trust for true emergencies only.

How far ahead should we book?

Most couples book 1–2 years out. If your date is closer, ask anyway.

What areas do you cover?

Greater Boston, the Portland, Maine area and the rest of New England.

How does booking work?

Send the form or email me. If I'm available, we'll set up a call. I'll email a contract, and a one-third deposit holds your date.

What if you're already booked?

I'll tell you right away and, if you'd like, recommend someone I trust.

Can we choose songs and make a do-not-play list?

Absolutely, from the prelude before the ceremony to the last song of the night. My wedding planner makes it easy to list key songs, must-plays and do-not-plays.

Do you take requests from guests?

Yes, and I check them against your preferences so the night stays yours.

Do you mix?

Yes. Song selection comes first, and then I blend tracks so the floor never loses momentum.

Can you work with live musicians?

Yes. I've shared the stage with soloists, saxophonists and a marching band, and I'll coordinate sound and timing with them.

Can you cover the ceremony and cocktail hour in different spaces?

Yes. I bring separate systems for each area, with wireless and lapel mics.

What if there's no power at the site?

I've run weddings off generators, and I have my own battery power for ceremonies and cocktail hours.

Do you have insurance?

Yes, a $1 million commercial policy. I'll provide a certificate of insurance for your venue on request.

What do you wear?

Whatever suits your event, a suit by default. Tell me if you'd like something more or less formal.

What equipment do you use, and do you bring backup?

Professional JBL and QSC sound systems, with a full backup on site at every event. The volume is set for the room: full on the dance floor, comfortable at the tables.

Can we reach you while we plan?

Please do. Email me anytime. I answer quickly.
