How it works

  1. 1

    Get in touch.

    Send the form or email me with your date and venue.

  2. 2

    Book.

    I'll send a contract; a one-third deposit holds the date.

  3. 3

    Plan.

    My wedding planner covers key songs, must-plays and do-not-plays. Then we walk through the timeline together.

  4. 4

    Dance.

    I arrive early, work with your venue and vendors, and keep the day on time and the floor full.

Services

  • Reception DJ and MC

    Music built around your taste, read live from the room. Announcements that are short, rehearsed and on time.

  • Ceremony and cocktail hour

    Separate systems for each space, wireless and lapel mics, and battery power for sites without outlets.

  • Lighting

    Battery-powered LED uplighting in any color, for rooms and tents. Dance lighting is available too.

  • Corporate and private events

    The same gear and the same approach for company parties, milestones and celebrations.
